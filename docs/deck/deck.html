Where it stands

Ran on testnet. Open, repay and close used these same amounts. All the BTC came back, for a net cost of about 2 MUSD.

Does it pay?

Not yet. A trove costs 1% a year. pMUSD pays more than that above about 39% utilisation, and pays 0.03% at today's 7%. The page shows both rates.

Mezo's rules stay in force

1,800 MUSD minimum net debt, 150% system ratio to open, no closing in Recovery Mode. EOA wallets only, because Mezo checks plain ECDSA.

8

What is not done, and when it should be.

Not done

  • No confirmed outside users. Every wallet on page 6 is ours. Tester guide: docs/TESTING.md
  • No audit. Unaudited contracts, on testnet only.
  • One owner key. It can change risk limits with no delay until the timelock arrives.
  • MEZO has one live price. If Mezo's push stops, MEZO borrows and liquidations revert until it returns.
  • The live demo is a dev server, so a first load can be slow.

Plan

  • By 12 November 2026 Outside testers.
  • December 2026 Static build. Timelock and multisig. MEZO price fallback in service.
  • First quarter of 2027 External audit, then mainnet with capped markets.

The later dates are plans, not commitments.
